6. Viscosity rescaling (10.22)-(10.23): u_nu(x,t) = nu^{1/2} u(x/nu^{1/2}, t).
   Energy scaling ||u_nu||_2^2 = nu^{5/2} ||u||_2^2 comes from nu^{1/2 * 2} times
   the Jacobian nu^{3/2}: exponent 1 + 3/2 = 5/2 (paper: 5/2).
   Dissipation: nu * |grad u_nu|^2 * dx scales as nu^{1} * nu^{1-1} * nu^{3/2}
   = nu^{5/2}, matching (10.23).
   Force derivatives: d_x^alpha d_t^m f_nu = nu^{(1-|alpha|)/2} (d_y^alpha d_t^m f).
     |alpha| = 0  ->  factor nu^{1/2}
     |alpha| = 1  ->  factor nu^{0}
     |alpha| = 2  ->  factor nu^{-1/2}
     |alpha| = 3  ->  factor nu^{-1}
   So spatial derivative norms of the force grow like nu^{-(|alpha|-1)/2} as
   nu -> 0, while the spatial support shrinks to K_nu = nu^{1/2} K and the time
   support K x [0,2] is unchanged.
