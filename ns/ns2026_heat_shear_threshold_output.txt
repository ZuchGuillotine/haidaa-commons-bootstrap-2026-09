Reading: the threshold is essentially independent of h (Z* about 0.45 for m=h/4
and about 1.0 for m=h/2), so the asserted inequality -Z Hcal'/Hcal < h/4 on the
outer collar is the concrete requirement X > about 4.4 d, i.e. a fixed lower bound
on the outer radius, NOT a further smallness condition on h.  Since the paper
places the collar at X >= e^{1/2} X_tail with X_tail > X_v > X_R = 110 (C P_*)^{10}
and P_* > e^{e^{M_d}+10}, the requirement is met with enormous room.
