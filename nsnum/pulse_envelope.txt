pulse_envelope.py -- reduced 1D model of the ring-pulse
growth-then-viscous-decay envelope in the 2026 forced Navier-Stokes
blow-up construction, and the one-slot amplification budget.
python 3.14.4  numpy 2.5.3  scipy 1.18.1
No random state; output is deterministic.
Nominal constants: lam_0 = 1, u_* = 1, slot length L_s = l^2.

==========================================================================
4. AMPLIFICATION BUDGET OVER ONE SLOT vs THE REQUIRED WAVE AMPLITUDE
==========================================================================
  Required: the stress matching needs a wave amplitude
    A_wave ~ q^(-1/2 - h/2) with q ~ Q = 2^(-l),
  i.e. ln A_wave = l (1/2 + h/2) ln 2, LINEAR in l.
  Available (this model): the growth half-slot supplies
    G_peak - G(0) = c_- L_s = c_- l^2, QUADRATIC in l.

  h = 1/200:
       l     required ln A_wave    available ln gain    available/required
       2               0.6966               0.4811                  0.69
       4               1.3932               1.9242                  1.38
       6               2.0898               4.3296                  2.07
       8               2.7865               7.6970                  2.76
      10               3.4831              12.0265                  3.45
      16               5.5729              30.7879                  5.52
      20               6.9661              48.1061                  6.91
      40              13.9323             192.4245                 13.81

  Reading. In this reduced model the available linear amplification over
  one slot exceeds the required amplitude for every l >= 2 and the
  ratio grows linearly in l, so the amplitude selection is not
  amplification-limited: a seed as small as exp(-c l^2) still reaches a
  fixed power of q within one slot. Equivalently, the model predicts
  the pulse budget is set by the two-sided exp(-c l^2) cutoff, not by
  a shortage of growth.

  FALSIFICATION CRITERION for a faithful implementation. Replace the
  nominal lam_0, u_* and L_s by the construction's own frame constants
  and background, and recompute G_peak - G(0). If that quantity is not
  at least l (1/2 + h/2) ln 2 for the l used in the construction --
  in particular if it is O(1) or O(log l) rather than growing at least
  linearly in l -- then the amplification available over one slot
  cannot supply the amplitude the stress matching requires, and the
  amplitude selection needs a different source. A shortfall must be
  reproduced by a second, independent implementation (for instance a
  frozen-annulus generalized eigenvalue problem) before it is reported
  as anything other than a property of this reduced model.
