  at b_s = 0 the admissible interval is |w| < sqrt(2/(a-2)) = 1.199896
  best w for given b_s is w* = -b_s/(a-2), giving
      m_max = 2 + 2 b_s^2/(a(a-2)),  e.g. b_s = 1 -> m_max = 2.424814
  so for every a > 2 there is a stress direction with margin >= 2;
  the cone condition constrains the DIRECTION w = p_2/p_1, not the
  existence of some admissible direction. This is why the audit that
  matters is the one that uses the profiles' own w, not a sweep.
