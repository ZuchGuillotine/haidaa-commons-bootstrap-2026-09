SECTION 0.2 -- where the asymptotic regime begins (h = 1/200)
==============================================================================
Small parameters of the construction, all powers of q^h with q ~ tau.

target                                          requires tau =     s = -ln tau
q^(2h) = 0.1  (background expansion small)             10^-100             230
Re_theta = tau^-h = 10                                 10^-200             461
q^(h/2) = 0.1 (pulses genuinely sub-core)              10^-400             921

IEEE-754 double underflow (smallest normal) is about 1e-308, so
tau = 1e-400 cannot be stored as a double at all; tau = 1e-100 and
tau = 1e-400 are s = 230 and s = 921, both exactly ordinary numbers.

State of the flow at the deepest tau a physical-space time-marching
computation could plausibly reach, tau = 1e-6, at h = 1/200:
  Re_theta = tau^-h                 = 1.0715
  aspect ratio l_z/l_r = tau^-h     = 1.0715
  q^(2h)  (expansion parameter)     = 0.8710
  q^(h/2) = l_wave/l_r              = 0.9661
  waves per core radius             = 1.0351
  amplitude excess over tau^-1/2    = 7.2 %

i.e. an O(1)-Reynolds-number viscous vortex with about one carrier
wavelength across the core: no scale separation of any kind.

Exterior: the exterior swirl obeys the 1D radial heat-type equation
-d_tau K = d_rr K + K_r/r - K/r^2, so it costs O(1e2) radial points as a
1D problem and must not be discretized as 3D Navier-Stokes.

CAVEAT on N_theta: the azimuthal estimate assumes the carrier is
isotropic, m ~ 2*pi*r/l_wave ~ tau^(-h/2), giving m_max = 6.5 at
tau = 1e-6. That is an INFERENCE, not a quotation from the paper.
If instead m ~ 1/l_wave ~ tau^-(1/2+h/2), N_theta and the 3D cost grow
by a further factor tau^-(1/2), i.e. about 1e3 at tau = 1e-6.

SECTION 1.5 -- exponent discrimination and the surrogate-h table
==============================================================================
The numerical claim to be discriminated is |u|_inf ~ tau^-(1/2+h)
against the ordinary Leray scaling tau^-(1/2). Over k decades of tau
the amplitude ratio is 10^(k*h).

     dynamic range    amplitude excess    log10 excess
         2 decades               2.33%          0.0100
         4 decades               4.71%          0.0200
         6 decades               7.15%          0.0300
        60 decades              99.53%          0.3000

So at h = 1/200 the measurement is a log-log slope discrimination of
  0.5000 vs 0.5050  -- a relative exponent difference of 1.0%.
Over 6 decades that is 0.030 in log10 amplitude, so a 5-sigma
measurement needs the TOTAL systematic error budget (spatial +
temporal truncation + outer-boundary/cutoff + initialization
transient + fitting-window bias) below about 0.3% in amplitude,
sustained over 6 decades.

Surrogate-h table, evaluated at tau = 1e-4:
         h    Re_theta    aspect   q^(h/2)   waves/core  slope 1/2+h
     0.005        1.05      1.05      0.98         1.02        0.505
     0.010        1.10      1.10      0.95         1.05        0.510
     0.050        1.58      1.58      0.79         1.26        0.550
     0.100        2.51      2.51      0.63         1.58        0.600
     0.150        3.98      3.98      0.50         2.00        0.650

h in (1/100, 1/6) is OUTSIDE the interval the proof's estimates
require (0 < h < 1/100) though inside the energy-integrability
constraint h < 1/6. A run at such h is a mechanism study, never a
verification of the theorem.
