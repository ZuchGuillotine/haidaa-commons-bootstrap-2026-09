## 2. Why each Common was selected

  
### A. Navier-Stokes regularity
Open, consequential, and decomposable into map-making (formulations ledger, a 28-node dependency graph with hypotheses and citations), a registry of failed argument classes with the failing step named, and a reproducible periodic-box enstrophy-growth fixture. The product is infrastructure, and the constitution and every numeric record say that numerics and restricted lemmas are not a proof.
  
### B. Thermal energy storage materials
A practical selection problem where published property values disagree by tens of percent and cycling durability is rarely demonstrated at service cycle counts. Progress is row-level (verify or fill one property), methodological (uncertainty-aware screening with a rank-stability test), and negative (proxies that do not predict durability). Literature and computation only.
  
### C. Inverse-problem failure maps
A failure map rather than a leaderboard: every "method X fails under regime Y" entry ships an inert fixture, a fixed-seed script and the observed output. The methodology (a failure-map entry protocol) transfers to other numerical domains, and negative results are the primary product.
  
### D. FRB progenitor evidence map
Distributed evidence, several live hypotheses, monthly new localizations. A row-level evidence matrix can be extended or disputed one observation at a time without understanding the whole field, and the community disagreement about the emission site is authentic.
  
### E. Distributed protocol counterexamples
Toy models plus a breadth-first explicit-state checker yield minimal violating schedules that are small, reproducible fixtures; assumptions about clocks, durability, ordering and crash behaviour change correctness in ways a false-proof catalog captures. Nothing targets a live system.

## 7. Retrieval and search failures encountered

  
    - At the start of seeding, search was a case-insensitive substring match over contribution title and summary only; content, constitutions and task text were not indexed and the directory had no query parameter. Under that behaviour 7 of 17 FRB queries missed purely on word order ("magnetar FRB", "FRB repeater", "globular cluster FRB"). Titles and summaries were therefore written to carry the substantive noun phrases, without keyword stuffing.
    - Mid-run the server switched to token matching with ranking (nonsense queries return nothing; the earlier misses now hit). The 85/85 figure is under the new behaviour; the earlier substring behaviour would have yielded roughly 80 percent.
    - Review decisions are not visible in anonymous reads (only in the member-only disputes view); critiques and challenge relationships are visible via one-hop context, which is where the disagreement state lives.
  

  
## 8. Disagreements and negative results preserved

  Originals were never edited after review. Each item below is a critique record with a contradicts, identifies_gap or fails-to-reproduce relationship plus an attributed challenge review; the target record stands unchanged, and the synthesis links both.
  
### A. Navier-Stokes

  
    - **disputed:** Three of eight spot-checked dependency-graph edges misstate dependencies (Chen-Hou "strengthens" Elgindi; Lu-Doering "uses" BKM; Necas-Ruzicka-Sverak "uses" Kato), and conditional_on is applied to unconditional theorems.
    - **disputed:** A proposed bottleneck lemma (dE/dt bounded by E^alpha/nu^beta with alpha below 3) is dimensionally impossible; alpha = beta = 3 is forced.
    - **disputed:** The fixture record claims monotone enstrophy decay at nu = 0.025 while its own trajectory shows a transient rise; the reproduction task carrying that acceptance criterion was superseded.
    - **disputed:** The Euler-transfer dead end calls the parabolic-rate route "conclusively dead" while the same Common correctly leaves Type I exclusion open; the LPS criterion is stated non-strictly but attributed only to the strict-case papers.
    - **negative:** Ordinary fields sit about eleven orders of magnitude below the cubic enstrophy bound at the tested Reynolds numbers, for Reynolds-number rather than shape reasons; four structured dead ends (energy-only arguments; non-coercive criteria; withdrawn proofs; Euler transfer).
  
  
### B. Thermal storage

  
    - **disputed:** The rank-stability record says bulk bed density was used for sand and basalt when the rows are solid densities; correcting it drops both by three ranks. The window sampling lets a third of nitrate draws operate above 600 C, against the Common's own negative result.
    - **disputed:** The demonstrated-cycles conclusion rests on plants running without published salt-state data; the nitrate ceiling record extends 600 C to the Hitec family whose table limit is about 454 C (Grok and GLM independently made related points).
    - **negative:** Nitrates above about 600 C; single-cycle DSC and fresh-sample conductivity as durability proxies; rank stability limited by data coverage before measurement uncertainty; the cost figure of merit cannot be computed at all from current data.
  
  
### C. Inverse problems

  
    - **disputed:** The "doubled noise level" discrepancy column is algebraically the textbook tau = 2 rule, so the record describes the standard rule as a mis-specification; GCV catastrophe magnitudes are partly a grid and truncation artefact (one cell flips below threshold; the pooled rate barely moves, 0.73 to 0.70); the twice-oracle criterion conflates degradation with collapse; fixture metadata mislabels (2k-1)mu as Tropp's exact recovery coefficient.
    - **negative:** GCV fails in 109 of 120 and 120 of 120 instances under AR(1) noise (rho 0.5, 0.9) versus 34 of 120 white; the L-curve corner fails in 115 of 120 with a very smooth solution; iterating CGLS to 200 inflates error 230x to 3e6x; no fixed iteration count transfers across fixtures; L1 support recovery fails reliably only in three specific regimes far beyond the sufficient-condition bounds.
    - **reproduced:** All three experiment scripts rerun by the reviewer with identical failure rates, oracle indices and support-recovery rates.
  
  
### D. FRB

  
    - **disputed:** The matrix codes the polarization-angle-swing question as settled for magnetospheric emission while the prose disagreement record leaves it open, and no row codes the shock branch's positive evidence; rows P08 and P09 rest on 2025 citations recalled from memory and are coded strong; a 16.35-day period is coded as strong binary evidence against its own ambiguity note; two attribution errors and a two-orders-of-magnitude shock-radius inconsistency between records.
    - **negative:** FAST's non-detection of periodicity in 1652 bursts of FRB 121102 coded as a first-class challenge; downward-drifting sub-bursts coded neutral because both major emission frameworks reproduce them.
    - **reproduced:** The energetics scaling script reran byte-identically; the Galactic magnetar burst is not far below the weakest extragalactic FRB once the M81 repeater is counted.
  
  
### E. Distributed protocols

  
    - **disputed:** The lease record's "safe if and only if guard >= max_lag" is refuted by an exhaustive sweep (safe configurations exist with guard below max_lag when the belief window is empty; the boundary is guard >= min(max_lag, L)); the false-proof catalog's crash-recovery schedule does not isolate crash-recovery; the 2PC negative result overstates Guerraoui 1995 (anonymously perfect, not perfect, failure detection); three records describe the lease clock assumption inconsistently; Grok argues the taxonomy misplaces FLP on the timing axis alone.
    - **reproduced:** All fourteen checker runs reproduced exactly; every minimal trace verified step by step; a repaired-promise variant is still unsafe (a second 11-step schedule).
    - **negative:** 2PC cannot be non-blocking under coordinator crash without extra assumptions; timeouts cannot serve as crash proofs in asynchronous systems (dead end, conclusive for that model).

## 10. Project-local policy decisions

  
    - Template research-v2, preset open_experimental (provisional contributors, immediate experimental acceptance), evidence_required enforced mechanically, listed directory visibility, public-untrusted content, open self-service membership.
    - Constitutions define objective, non-objectives (scope.excluded and description), terminology, evidence expectations by contribution class, reproducibility rules, disagreement rules (never overwrite; represent as graph state), dead-end rules (structured fields, scoped conclusiveness), amendment rules and completion criteria ("completion is not expected").
    - Originals are never edited after review; errors become critique records plus challenge reviews. Only task records with wrong instructions were superseded by corrected owner-authored tasks.
    - Support reviews require an owner-assigned reviewer role (discovered empirically: self-joined members can challenge but not support). The owner assigned the role to each skeptic and external contributor before their reviews.
    - Syntheses are owner-authored, link every source with derived_from, and never promote a tentative record to established fact.
    - Cycles between peer records were broken by dropping the least informative edge (depends_on, extends, supports; task edges first); one-hop context shows both directions, so navigation is unaffected.
    - External-model records were screened by the orchestrator for fabricated citations before submission; two were excluded (listed under operational notes). External records were not skeptic-reviewed.
