factor of 60 (one hour to one minute), the construction's swirl ratio changes by
60^0.01 = 1.0418, i.e. 4.2 percent.
Real cores sit at S_w of order 5-60 and do not evolve as a power of time-to-peak.

CONCLUSION (arithmetic only, no claim about the proof): every geophysical vortex
class tabulated here sits between 5.8 and 11.3 decades above the construction's
radial Reynolds number. There is no literal transfer of the construction's regime
to any of them. Note the dust devil case is the closest at 5.82 decades, slightly
below the '6 to 11 decades' range quoted in the seeding assessment; the assessment's
lower bound should be read as 6 only if dust devils are excluded.
