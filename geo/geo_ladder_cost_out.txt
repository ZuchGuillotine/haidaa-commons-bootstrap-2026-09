the project's posted pooled request is 80000 core-hours, leaving 13576 core-hours
of headroom (17%). The estimate is consistent with the posted request.

what would break this estimate, in order of leverage:
  1. tau_pt: linear. A measured tau_pt of 1e-5 instead of 4e-06 would give
     166059 core-hours and would exceed the posted request.
  2. adding a fifth rung at Delta = 6.25 m: adds 134218 core-hours per
     closure-swirl member, 966368 in total. Not affordable inside the request.
  3. a supercell-embedded member rather than a closed idealized domain:
     the domain volume grows by roughly 10^2-10^3, which is a separate request.

Implementing the azimuthal-mean/eddy accumulation INSIDE the model, rather than
diagnosing it from archived snapshots, reduces the storage requirement by a
factor of 13. That software task is therefore the highest-leverage single
item in the project, and it is a laptop-scale job, not a compute-block job.
